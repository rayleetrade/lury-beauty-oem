# Catalog content notes

The catalog contains 100 proposed made-to-order design directions across 10 style families. REF- identifiers distinguish these directions from the original W01-W04 / B01-B04 sample references.

Ten original AI-generated family illustrations are shared across the directions. They are not factory photographs, exact SKU photographs or evidence of stock availability. Generation prompts are recorded in style-illustration-prompts.json. Final specifications and appearance require physical sample approval.

PBT and PET are materials to request, not verified compositions of every illustrated design. Faux-mink, silk-effect, wispy, volume and brown describe a finish, shape or color and must not be counted as distinct raw materials. Other materials can be discussed against a buyer brief.

The owner supplied a typical bulk MOQ of 2,000 boxes. Per-style minimum, mixed-style eligibility, packaging minimums, prices, wear duration, reuse count, lead time and certification are not established for these proposed designs.

Independent retail links support the availability of related formats in named markets. They do not establish popularity rankings, the supplier's capabilities or endorsement by those retailers. All new formats are proposed with pre-applied adhesive and without a separate liquid-glue product.

Pages retain indexable text, canonical URLs, organization contact data and a sitemap. These support discoverability but cannot guarantee search rankings, AI citations or display of WhatsApp details in search results.
